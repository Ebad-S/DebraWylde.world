The turning point

You know something needs to change.

From the outside, you may be doing well.

You have experience. You’ve achieved things. You know how to make things happen.

But what got you here no longer feels big enough for where you’re going.

There’s an idea you can’t let go of.

A contribution you want to make.

A different way you want to work, lead or build.

You know there’s more you’re here to do.

The problem isn’t the ambition.

It’s working out how to turn it into a business.

Recognise this?

Maybe this is where you are.

  • You can feel the call, but you’re terrified to follow it.Changing direction could put income, security or a hard-earned professional identity at risk.
  • You know you need to transition, but don’t know how.You need a path forward that doesn’t require blowing up your current life first.
  • Your message isn’t landing.You know your work is valuable. Your market doesn’t understand it quickly enough.
  • You’re undercharging.Your expertise has grown. Your pricing hasn’t grown with it.
  • Your business is too small for your vision.Your current offers or model simply aren’t capable of creating the income or impact you want.
  • You have too many ideas and no clear commercial direction.You need to decide what the business actually is, and what it isn’t.
  • You’re successful, but no longer fulfilled by the work.The old version of success has stopped being enough.
  • You’ve outgrown your positioning.The way you’ve described yourself no longer reflects the level or value of the work you want to do.
  • You keep waiting for certainty.But clarity isn’t arriving while you stand still.
  • You know there’s more in you, but you haven’t made space for it yet.Your current commitments, responsibilities and ways of working leave little room to explore what the next chapter could actually become.

You don’t need another idea.
You need a path.

Vision, grounded

Purpose isn’t a business model.

Knowing you’re here to do meaningful work is powerful.

But purpose alone doesn’t tell you:

  • Who will buy it.
  • How to position it.
  • What to offer.
  • What to charge.
  • How you’ll make money.
  • Or how to transition without unnecessarily putting your financial security at risk.

That’s the work of Align & Thrive.

We take the bigger vision and bring it down to earth.

  • Who are you here to serve?
  • What problem are you uniquely equipped to solve?
  • What are you going to sell?
  • How will you communicate its value?
  • What will you charge?
  • What business model can support the life, wealth and impact you want?

And crucially:

What needs to change in you to lead it?

The shift

Alignment becomes commercial.

Alignment isn’t about sitting around waiting for something to feel perfect.

It’s knowing what matters enough to make better decisions.

When you’re aligned:

  • You stop trying to sell work you’ve outgrown.
  • You stop explaining a message nobody understands.
  • You stop pricing from an old version of yourself.
  • You stop chasing every possible opportunity.
  • You become clearer about your market, your value and where the money comes from.
  • And you start building around the contribution you’re actually here to make.

The goal isn’t purpose instead of prosperity.
It’s a business capable of creating both.

Alignment. Wealth. Impact.

Five weeks. One clear business path.

  1. 01

    The Signal

    What is trying to change?

    We identify what’s no longer working and what the frustration, restlessness or bigger idea is actually telling you.

    You’ll separate the real issue from the symptoms and identify where your current business, career or identity no longer fits.

    OutcomeClarity on what needs to change, and why.

  2. 02

    Alignment

    What’s keeping you where you are?

    Fear. Security. Beliefs. Identity. Expectations.

    We’ll identify what’s influencing your decisions and where you’re holding onto a version of yourself or your business you’ve already outgrown.

    Then we’ll start making decisions from where you’re going, not where you’ve been.

    OutcomeClearer decisions and the confidence to move.

  3. 03

    The Vision

    What are you actually building?

    We turn the bigger call into something concrete.

    • Your contribution.
    • Your ideal audience.
    • The problem you’re here to solve.
    • The work you want to be known for.
    • The life you want the business to support.
    • And the level of wealth you want it to create.

    OutcomeA defined direction for your next business chapter.

  4. 04

    The Business Path

    Make it commercially viable.

    This is where vision becomes business.

    Positioning. Messaging. Audience. Offers. Pricing. Revenue model. Path to market.

    You’ll identify what needs to be built, changed or removed to create a business capable of carrying the bigger vision.

    OutcomeA commercially grounded business model you can act on.

  5. 05

    Build What’s Next

    Turn the strategy into action.

    No 47-point business plan. We’ll decide what matters now.

    • What stays.
    • What goes.
    • What gets built first.
    • Where your first revenue opportunities sit.
    • And what can wait.

    You’ll turn the work into a focused 90-day action plan.

    OutcomeClear priorities, commercial actions and your next 90 days mapped out.

Experience is your foundation

You’re not starting from zero.

You’re bringing years, perhaps decades, of experience with you.

  • Your expertise.
  • Your relationships.
  • Your reputation.
  • Your successes.
  • Your failures.
  • Your judgement.
  • Your hard-won wisdom.

The opportunity isn’t to throw it all away and reinvent yourself.

It’s to reorganise everything you’ve learned around the work you’re now here to do.

That’s very different from starting again.

You’re building from experience.

Tangible direction

What you’ll leave with

Not another folder of ideas. A business path you can actually follow.

  • What you’re buildingand why.
  • Who it’s forand the problem you’re solving.
  • How to positionand communicate your value.
  • What you can selland how it makes money.
  • Where your pricingmay need to change.
  • What needs to shiftin your current business or career.
  • What has been holdingyou back personally.
  • What to focus onover the next 90 days.
